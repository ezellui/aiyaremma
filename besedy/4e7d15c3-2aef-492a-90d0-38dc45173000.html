The two-step workflow proved most productive, yielding four named concept proposals ("Night Archivist," "Cold Field Cartographer," "Laboratory Calibrator," "Polychromatic Systematizer"), with Concept 4 selected as the basis for further iteration.

The final successful result came from combining Concept 4's structure (neutral graphite shell, many separated layers, stepped architectural silhouette, direct evaluating gaze) with targeted image edits: first adding branching seam lines connecting colored rivets into cluster groupings, then attempting a lighting edit for nocturnal atmosphere. The person rejected the lighting edit, judging that darkness obscured visual information and added theatrical rather than functional mood — directly citing the source aesthetic's principle that "darkness should serve the data, not become theatrical." The person accepted the first edit (seam connections) as the stopping point. Claude agreed and noted two remaining imperfections: rivets rendered as glossy beads rather than flat matte enamel studs, and color groups forming chains rather than true branching clusters, offering an optional further edit prompt for these. Стиль скорее поздние 90-е и нулевые (CLAMP) в современном цифровом рендере, а не целлулоидная анимация середины 90-х.
