A vertical baroque philosophical poster in the same style as a 19th-century 
German Romantic frontispiece crossed with anime sentai illustration. Title 
at the top in monumental serif capitals: "SAILOR WELTGEIST", with subtitle 
below: "GUARDIAN OF THE ABSOLUTE". Beneath the subtitle, the full Hegelian 
formula in two lines, smaller serif italic German: "WAS VERNÜNFTIG IST, DAS 
IST WIRKLICH; / UND WAS WIRKLICH IST, DAS IST VERNÜNFTIG."

Central figure: a young woman in her early twenties standing tall in a 
commanding, slightly heroic frontal pose, gaze directed UPWARD AND OUTWARD, 
PAST the viewer, into historical distance — she does not meet the viewer's 
eye; she watches history happen. Her expression is serene, weighty, 
self-aware. Long flowing dark hair lifted faintly as if by an unseen wind 
of history.

Palette: deep imperial black background, heavy ornamental gold (the gold of 
crowns and sunsets, not jewelry), accents of imperial red in the cape lining 
and skirt — the colors of the Holy Roman Empire and of the Hegelian dusk 
(Minerva's owl flying at twilight). Rich tenebrist contrast.

Costume: a sailor-senshi-style uniform in black and burgundy with elaborate 
gold filigree, a high pointed collar, gold cuffs, an ornate pleated skirt 
with red and gold panels, knee-high black boots with gold trim. A long heavy 
half-cape in deep red lined with darker burgundy, worn over the left shoulder 
and falling to the ground. The cape's interior surface is broad and flat 
enough to display long German inscriptions in elegant gold serif lettering.

A central medallion at the chest: a golden sun-eye, radiant, geometric, with 
rays — the absolute viewpoint from which Spirit recognizes itself. A tiara 
above the brow with a small third-eye motif and a single ruby at center. 
Gold earrings, white opera-length gloves.

In her left hand, held forward and slightly elevated: an ornate intertwined 
RING-TRISKELION made of three interlocking metal rings — gold, dark steel, 
and red enamel — fused together as a single unbreakable triple knot, sparking 
with golden light. This is the dialectical triad as An-sich / Für-sich / 
An-und-für-sich, made visible.

Behind her head: a vast cosmic spiral of golden light radiating outward — 
the Absolute as self-mediated infinity, with a bright central sun.

CAPE INSCRIPTIONS: the open red interior of the cape carries two long German 
quotations in elegant gold serif, framed with small ornaments:
- Left side of the cape: "DIE WAHRHEIT IST DAS GANZE. — DAS GANZE ABER IST 
  NUR DAS DURCH SEINE ENTWICKLUNG SICH VOLLENDENDE WESEN."
- Right side of the cape: "DIE WELTGESCHICHTE IST DER FORTSCHRITT IM 
  BEWUSSTSEIN DER FREIHEIT. — FREIHEIT IST DIE EINSICHT IN DIE NOTWENDIGKEIT."

BORDER COMPOSITION — the seven historical stages of freedom according to 
Hegel's "Lectures on the Philosophy of History", arranged as ornate 
rectangular panels around the central figure, three on each side and one at 
the bottom-center, each labeled with elegant German titles and a brief 
formula of freedom:

Left column, top to bottom:
- "1. ÄGYPTEN — Einer ist frei." Scene: a colossal pharaoh-statue beside a 
  pyramid at dusk, slaves and priests at its base, monumental and oppressive.
- "2. PERSIEN — Einige sind frei." Scene: a Persian king on his throne 
  before a sacred fire, courtiers bowing, theocratic grandeur.
- "3. GRIECHENLAND — Einige sind frei, als freie." Scene: white marble 
  temples, philosophers in robes conversing among slaves laboring in the 
  background, dawn light.

Right column, top to bottom:
- "4. ROM — Alle sind frei als Personen." Scene: Roman legionaries before 
  the Senate house, the SPQR standard rising, civic-legal order.
- "5. CHRISTLICHE EUROPA — Die Freiheit ist in das Innere gefasst." Scene: 
  a Gothic cathedral interior with stained glass, a single monk reading at 
  a desk by candlelight, inwardness and conscience.
- "6. FRANZÖSISCHE REVOLUTION — Die Freiheit für alle." Scene: a tricolor 
  flag held aloft, Marianne-figure leading a crowd, smoke and dawn behind 
  her.

Bottom-center, between the two lower corner scenes:
- "7. DEUTSCHLAND — Die Freiheit im Begriff." Scene: a stern Hegel-figure at 
  the lecture podium in Berlin, students taking notes, a chalkboard reading 
  "Philosophie — Wissenschaft des Begriffs". This panel is slightly larger 
  and visually weighted as the synthesis of the two corner scenes flanking 
  it.

BOTTOM CORNERS — explicitly framed as a theory/praxis pair, with identical 
oval frames, identical-size labels, visually equal rank:

- Bottom left, framed scene labeled "THEORIA": a small ornate diagram on 
  parchment showing a circle with three nodes — labeled "AN SICH", "FÜR 
  SICH", "AN UND FÜR SICH" — connected by curved arrows forming a single 
  triadic motion, with the word "AUFHEBUNG" in the center. Below the 
  diagram, smaller text: "Die Bewegung des Begriffs". (This is the Greek 
  contemplative moment: dialectic as pure thought.)

- Bottom right, framed scene labeled "PRAXIS": Napoleon Bonaparte on a 
  rearing white horse at the gates of Jena, sword raised, the world-spirit 
  on horseback. Below the scene, smaller text quoting Hegel's letter to 
  Niethammer: "Den Weltgeist zu Pferde gesehen". (This is the French 
  practical moment: Spirit acting in history.)

The Greek panel ("3. Griechenland") on the left edge and the French 
Revolution panel ("6. Französische Revolution") on the right edge should 
each be visually connected by a faint vertical line of golden filigree to 
their respective bottom-corner scenes — Greek philosophy descending into 
Theoria, French revolution descending into Praxis — making the vertical 
columns read as two parallel histories that the central German panel synthesizes.

Bottom of the poster, in monumental serif capitals: "DIE PHILOSOPHIE IST IHRE 
ZEIT IN GEDANKEN ERFASST. — G. W. F. HEGEL". Bottommost banner across the 
full width, small caps: "DER GEIST ERSCHEINT IN DER WELT, UM SICH SELBST ZU 
ERKENNEN."

Style: ornate German Romantic engraving aesthetic with heavy gold filigree 
and tenebrist chiaroscuro, photorealistic anime figure design, imperial 
black and gold and burgundy palette, vertical poster format, dense 
composition, museum print quality, atmosphere of historical solemnity at the 
twilight of an epoch.
