Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character,
90s Japanese anime aesthetic, cel-shaded line art.

Character: extremely thin, emaciated young woman with enormous pale eyes

Pose: crouching but dignified — like a broken queen
who still remembers she was once something else.
