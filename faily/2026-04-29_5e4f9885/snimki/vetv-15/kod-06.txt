Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded line art, 
large expressive eyes, severe and austere design.

DELIBERATE CONTRAST: unlike typical maximalist magical girl aesthetics,
this character is intentionally minimal and restrained — 
this austerity is itself a philosophical statement.

Character: young woman with short dark severe hair, 
intense searching expression, slight frown of concentration.

Uniform: steel grey and cold white magical warrior outfit, 
almost monastic in simplicity — structured collar, minimal ornamentation,
thin silver trim only. White gloves. Grey boots. 
No sparkles. No frills. This is intentional.

SPLIT DESIGN — two halves of the costume visible:
Left half: Tractatus-era — rigid geometric lines, 
small numbered propositions (1.0, 1.1, 2.0...) 
embroidered faintly along the hem.
Right half: Investigations-era — fluid organic lines, 
small overlapping language-game bubbles along the sleeve.

Brooch: not a gem — a small blackboard with the equation 
"7 = ?" erasing and rewriting itself.

Weapon: a luminous ladder made of pure white light,
held in one hand — but the bottom rungs are already dissolving,
disappearing as if used and discarded.

Expression: the posture of someone who has just realized
the question itself was wrong.

Background: blackboard covered in numbered propositions
(Tractatus style) on one side, dissolving into 
overlapping family-resemblance diagrams and language-game bubbles
on the other side. Austrian village schoolroom visible faintly.

At the bottom: bold austere title — "SAILOR GRENZE"
Subtitle in smaller script: "Die Grenzen meiner Sprache 
bedeuten die Grenzen meiner Welt"

Color palette: steel grey, cold white, silver, 
pale chalk-white glow, minimal gold — deliberately restrained.

Style: vintage Japanese magical girl anime illustration,
clean linework, the visual austerity itself communicates character philosophy.
