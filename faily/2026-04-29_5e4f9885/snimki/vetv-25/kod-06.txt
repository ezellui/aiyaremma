Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded line art,
large expressive eyes, dramatic pose.

Character: young woman in a magical warrior uniform —
black base with bright red tartan/checkered pattern on the cape,
white sailor collar, white gloves, black knee-high boots
with red trim.

Instead of a tiara: a small black bowler hat
perched slightly sideways on short dark hair.

Weapon: a tiny worn pencil stub held like a scepter,
glowing with dark energy. Around it: faint outlines
of figures mid-fall, dissolving into nothing.

Expression: completely deadpan. Not sad, not happy —
the expression of someone who has seen everything
and found it mildly interesting.

Background: deep black void. But populated with:
falling chairs, flying cats, pages of manuscript
torn and scattered, a single window showing
a brick wall, sequences of numbers dissolving
into nonsense — all arranged with eerie perfect symmetry.

A small black cat sits at her feet, staring directly
at the viewer with human-like intelligence.

Poster layout: large vertical format.
Title at bottom in bold constructivist-style lettering:
"SAILOR CASUS"
Subtitle beneath: "Хранительница Разрыва Причинности"

A tiny footnote at the very bottom edge:
"Один упал. Второй упал."

Color palette: deep black, bright red, 
dirty white, yellow accent, 
one random bright teal object that has no explanation.

Style: vintage Japanese magical girl anime illustration,
clean linework, flat cel shading,
constructivist typography elements mixed with magical girl ornaments,
Soviet avant-garde decorative borders.
Collectible poster composition.
