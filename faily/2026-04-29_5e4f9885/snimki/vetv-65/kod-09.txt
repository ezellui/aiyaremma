Anime character sheet poster, 90s magical girl aesthetic.

transitioning to the first light at edges,
gold and silver nebulae forming from musical notation.
Minimalist silver frame with tengwar star-border.

LAYOUT — infographic style with decorated panels, Russian text labels.
Panels have a different quality than usual — 
slightly translucent, as if the world shows through them.

TOP CENTER: large full-body illustration —
white and Laurelin-gold uniform, silver trim,
extraordinarily long silver-white hair with gold threads,
three Silmarils orbiting slowly: white, gold, deep red.
Absolute stillness in posture. Eyes like pre-stellar void.
