Color palette: black, deep crimson, excessive gold,
pale ghost-glow from cracking globe.
One exception: the globe-dance panel glows
soft blue — the only honest moment,
the only genuine color in the entire sheet.

Style: 90s anime cel-shading, pompous totalitarian
art deco typography, Russian text throughout,
Double Cross decorative dividers between panels,
deliberately over-ornamented frame —
the design itself is too much, like her.

CRITICAL CHARACTER NOTE for all illustrations:
The character is female. Feminine figure, feminine face.
Small toothbrush mustache is present in every image —
not as a joke or disguise,
but simply as part of her appearance.
No one in the poster comments on this.
It is completely unremarkable.
This tension is the entire point.
