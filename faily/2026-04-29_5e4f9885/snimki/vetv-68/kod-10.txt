Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded lineart,
dramatic theatrical lighting.

Character: a female magical girl warrior embodying
the pompous dictator character Adenoid Hynkel
from a 1940s satirical film.
The character is unambiguously female —
slender figure, feminine face —
but with slicked-back dark hair,
a small toothbrush mustache,
and an exaggerated military dictator uniform
transformed into a flamboyant magical girl battle outfit.

The mustache is not a costume element.
It is simply part of her face.
She does not find this unusual.

Outfit: deep black magical girl uniform
with a short pleated skirt,
excessive gold epaulettes on the shoulders,
crimson sash across chest covered in
absurd oversized medals and a Double Cross insignia
(two horizontal bars ✝✝) as the central brooch.
White elbow-length gloves, tall black boots with gold trim.
The uniform is simultaneously a sailor fuku and a dictator's dress uniform.
Too many medals. Too much gold. This is intentional.

Tiara: ornate gold headpiece with Double Cross emblem at center
instead of a gemstone.

Weapon: a large luminous globe —
translucent crystal sphere the size of a beach ball,
floating and slowly rotating beside her.
She balances it on one white-gloved finger
with theatrical, practiced grace.

Pose: maximally grandiose —
one arm extended toward the sky,
chin lifted, the posture of someone
declaring ownership of everything in view.
But the globe has a hairline crack glowing faintly gold.

Expression: absolute self-certainty
masking total emptiness.
The eyes of someone who has never questioned herself.

Background: vast red-draped Tomainian palace balcony
dissolving into a cosmic void.
Below — an infinite crowd rendered as a single blurry mass.
Above — stars that are indifferent.
Scattered: torn speeches in gibberish script,
broken medals floating like debris.

Poster layout: large vertical format,
title at bottom in pompous gothic art-deco lettering:
"SAILOR TOMANIA"
subtitle: "Хранительница Мирового Господства"

Color palette: black, crimson, excessive gold,
pale ghostly glow from the cracking globe.

Style: vintage Japanese magical girl anime illustration,
theatrical cel-shading, pompous decorative frame
with totalitarian art deco motifs —
deliberately over-ornamented, deliberately too much.
