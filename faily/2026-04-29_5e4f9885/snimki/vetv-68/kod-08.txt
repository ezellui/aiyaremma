Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded lineart,
dramatic theatrical lighting.

Character: a pompous male-presenting magical warrior
based on the character Adenoid Hynkel from a 1940s satirical film.
Slicked-back dark hair, small toothbrush mustache,
exaggerated military dictator uniform transformed into
a flamboyant magical girl battle outfit.

Outfit: deep black uniform with excessive gold epaulettes,
crimson sash across chest covered in absurd medals and
a Double Cross insignia (two horizontal bars, ✝✝) as brooch.
White gloves, tall black boots with gold trim.
The uniform is simultaneously imposing and faintly ridiculous —
too many medals, too much gold.

Tiara: ornate gold headpiece with Double Cross emblem at center.

Weapon: a large luminous globe — translucent crystal sphere
the size of a beach ball, floating and slowly rotating.
He balances it on one finger with theatrical grace.

Pose: grandiose — one arm extended toward the sky,
chin lifted, the posture of a man declaring ownership of everything.
But the globe is visibly beginning to crack.

Expression: absolute self-certainty masking total emptiness.
The eyes of someone who has never questioned himself.

Background: vast red-draped Tomainian palace balcony
dissolving into a cosmic void. Below — an infinite crowd
rendered as a single blurry mass. Above — stars that do not care.
Scattered fragments: torn speeches in gibberish script,
broken medals floating like debris.

Poster layout: large vertical format,
title at bottom in pompous gothic-art-deco lettering:
"SAILOR TOMANIA"
subtitle in smaller script: "Хранитель Мирового Господства"

Color palette: black, crimson, excessive gold,
pale ghostly glow from the cracking globe.

Style: vintage Japanese magical girl anime illustration,
theatrical cel-shading, pompous decorative frame
with totalitarian art deco motifs — 
deliberately over-ornamented, deliberately too much.
