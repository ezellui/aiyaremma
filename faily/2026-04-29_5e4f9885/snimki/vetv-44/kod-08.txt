Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic, cel-shading, dramatic noir lighting.

Character: tall, lean woman with sharp elegant features,
short dark hair slicked back or precisely cut,
expression utterly composed — not serene, calculated.
The stillness of someone who has already decided everything.

structured jacket silhouette reimagined as a sailor uniform,

no longer quite a weapon, not quite a wand.
Held loosely at her side, pointed down.
She doesn't need to raise it to be dangerous.

MI6 building bleeding into star formations,
a single shattered martini glass floating mid-air,
black rose petals drifting in zero gravity,
ghost silhouettes of the lost barely visible behind her —

Poster layout: vertical format,
bold title at bottom: "SAILOR SOVEREIGN"

Style: vintage anime illustration, clean precise linework,
noir atmosphere — a magical girl poster
deliberately drained of warmth.
The beauty is there. The warmth is not.
