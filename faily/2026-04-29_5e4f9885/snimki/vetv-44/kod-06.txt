Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior — 
but reimagined as a stoic male warrior in formal style.
90s anime aesthetic, cel-shading, dramatic lighting.

Character: tall, lean man with sharp cold features,
short dark hair, composed expression —
not heroic, just precise.

Uniform: black and charcoal tuxedo-inspired magical warrior outfit,
white shirt visible at the collar, silver trim,
black gloves. Not flamboyant — severe and elegant.
Small silver "00" insignia on the chest brooch
replacing the usual magical girl gem.

Weapon: a Walther PPK transformed into a crystalline object —
still recognizable as a pistol but made of dark glass,
no longer quite a weapon, not quite a wand.
Held loosely at the side, pointed down.

Expression: calm. Not serene — empty.
The composure of someone who has already calculated the cost.

Background: rainy London cityscape dissolving into deep space,
MI6 building blending into star formations,
a single martini glass floating shattered mid-air,
rose petals (black, not red) drifting in zero gravity,
ghost silhouettes of women behind him — Vesper, M —
barely visible, like smoke.

Color palette: black, charcoal, silver, cold steel blue,
single accent of deep crimson — controlled, minimal.

Poster layout: vertical format,
title at bottom: "SAILOR SOVEREIGN"
subtitle: "What must be done, will be done."

Style: vintage anime illustration,
clean linework, noir atmosphere,
the aesthetic of magical girl poster
but drained of warmth deliberately.
