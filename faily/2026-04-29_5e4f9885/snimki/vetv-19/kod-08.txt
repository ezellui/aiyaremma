Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded line art,
large expressive eyes, dramatic lighting.

Character: pale and slight, with dark melancholic eyes 
and disheveled black hair.
Expression: perpetual ironic half-smile concealing profound sorrow.

Uniform: deep charcoal black and dark crimson sailor uniform,
gold trim, structured collar. White gloves.
Tall black boots. A small broken mirror brooch at the chest —
cracked but still reflecting light.

Weapon: a long black quill pen that doubles as a staff,
tip glowing with dark violet ink-light that writes
symbols in the air as it moves.

Cape: asymmetrical — one half black, one half white,
stitched together unevenly along the spine.
On the black half: embroidered "ELLER" (Danish: "OR"),
on the white half: "ELLER" again —
Either/Or has no privileged side.

Background: foggy Copenhagen harbor dissolving into cosmic void,
crowds of faceless silhouettes receding into fog,
a single small figure standing apart — glowing — the Single Individual.
Scattered open journals with handwritten pseudonyms:
Johannes Climacus, Anti-Climacus, Victor Eremita.

Three small floating masks around the figure:
an aesthetic mask (pleasure), an ethical mask (duty),
a religious mask (fear and trembling) —
all slightly different from the real face beneath.
