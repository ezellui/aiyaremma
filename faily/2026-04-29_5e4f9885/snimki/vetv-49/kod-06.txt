Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded line art.

Character: extremely pale, gaunt-faced young woman
with dark circles under wide, haunted eyes.
Angular features, expression of profound existential dread
mixed with reluctant determination.
Dark hair, slightly disheveled.

Uniform: black and sickly phosphorescent green magical warrior outfit,
parchment-yellow trim, structured collar suggesting antiquarian formality.
White gloves visibly trembling.

Tiara: Elder Sign symbol as the gem — five-pointed star in a circle,
glowing with unsettling green light.

Weapon: a hovering ancient tome — the Necronomicon —
floating open beside her, pages turning on their own,
tentacle-like bookmarks curling outward.

Posture: half-heroic pose, half-cowering —
she is technically ready to fight but clearly does not want to be here.

Background: deep cosmic void with non-Euclidean geometry patterns,
cyclopean stone ruins, tentacles emerging from the darkness far away,
faint outlines of impossible architecture (R'lyeh),
scattered manuscript pages with indecipherable symbols.

Small detail: a cat sitting calmly at her feet,
utterly unbothered.

Poster layout: large vertical format,
title at bottom in ornate gothic lettering:
"SAILOR COSMICISM"
subtitle: "Guardian of the Threshold of the Unknowable"
small text beneath: "She would really rather not."

Color palette: deep black, phosphorescent sickly green,
parchment yellow, deep purple, cold starlight white.

Style: vintage Japanese magical girl anime illustration,
detailed linework, dramatic lighting,
