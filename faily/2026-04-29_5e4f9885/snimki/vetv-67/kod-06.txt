Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded line art,
large expressive eyes, dramatic pose.

Character: young woman in a Soviet-inspired magical warrior uniform —
deep crimson red and dark steel-grey, gold star emblem on the chest brooch,
structured military-collar silhouette with sailor elements,
white gloves, knee-high boots with red cuffs.

Hair: dark, severe, slicked back with a single dramatic wave —
deliberately androgynous, powerful.

Accessory: small golden five-pointed star tiara,
not decorative — functional, like a rank insignia.

Weapon: a long ornate pipe transforming into a pendulum clock scepter,
the pendulum swings slowly, inevitably.

Expression: absolute calm. Not cruel — certain.
The expression of someone who has decided the future.

Background: deep red-black cosmic void,
Soviet constructivist geometric patterns dissolving into stars,
blueprint schematics of factories and dams floating like manuscripts,
wheat fields becoming constellations.

Atmosphere: somewhere between a propaganda poster
and a magical girl transformation card.
The two aesthetics should fight each other and both win.

bold title at bottom: "SAILOR STALINIUM"

Color palette: Soviet crimson, dark steel, matte gold,

Style: vintage Japanese magical girl anime illustration
