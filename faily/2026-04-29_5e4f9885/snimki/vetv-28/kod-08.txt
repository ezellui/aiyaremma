Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded line art,
large expressive eyes, deeply melancholic atmosphere.

Character: young woman in a magical warrior uniform —
deep prussian blue and black with tarnished silver trim,
structured sailor-collar silhouette, pale almost translucent skin,
violet-blue eyes with the stillness of someone who has already seen everything.
Expression: not despair — the quietness after despair.

Hair: dark, long, loose, autumn leaves caught in it.
Her reflection in any surface shows a slightly different girl —
same face, different angle, the sister who is also her.

Tiara: black thorn-crown with a single cracked blue gem.

Weapon: a dried branch that blooms and wilts simultaneously —
white flowers opening on one end, ash and embers on the other.
Thin blue smoke rising.

Cape: long prussian blue, semi-transparent at edges,
dissolving into autumn fog and dead leaves at the hem.
In the fog of the cape — a second silhouette, barely visible,
same posture, same girl, half a step behind.

Background: the blue hour — not night, not day,
that specific twilight Trakl wrote about obsessively.
Bare black trees. A white deer half-visible in the treeline, watching.
Distant artillery flashes on the horizon as faint cold gold light.
Scattered morphine ampules glowing faintly like fallen stars.

Poster layout: vertical format, ornate art nouveau frame
with decay motifs — cracked gold, moth wings, bare branches.
Bold title at bottom: "SAILOR VERFALL"
subtitle in smaller gothic script: "Hüterin der Dämmerung"

Color palette: prussian blue, black, bone white,
tarnished gold, dark arterial red used sparingly.
The blue is sick, not beautiful.

Style: vintage Japanese magical girl anime illustration
crossed with German expressionist sensibility,
detailed linework, melancholic dramatic lighting,
collectible poster composition.
