Anime poster illustration, full-body magical girl warrior character.
90s Japanese anime aesthetic: clean cel-shaded line art,
large expressive eyes, dramatic and melancholic atmosphere.

Character: young man in a magical warrior uniform —
deep prussian blue and black with tarnished silver trim,
structured sailor-collar silhouette, pale almost translucent skin,
dark circles under violet-blue eyes, expression of quiet exhaustion
not despair but the stillness after despair.

Hair: dark, slightly disheveled, autumn leaves caught in it.

Tiara: black thorn-crown instead of tiara,
with a single cracked blue gem at center.

Weapon: a dried branch that blooms and wilts simultaneously —
flowers opening on one end, ash and embers on the other.
Faint blue smoke rising from it.

Cape: long, dark prussian blue, semi-transparent at the edges,
dissolving into autumn fog and dead leaves at the hem.

Background: deep twilight — not night, not day,
that specific blue hour Trakl wrote about endlessly.
Bare black trees. A white deer half-visible in the tree line,
watching. Distant artillery flashes on the horizon
rendered as faint gold light, not explosions.
Scattered morphine ampules glowing faintly like fallen stars.
A woman's silhouette — sister-shadow — fading into the treeline.

Poster layout: vertical format, ornate art nouveau frame
with decay motifs — cracked gold, moth wings.
Bold title at bottom: "SAILOR VERFALL"
subtitle in smaller gothic script: "Hüterin der Dämmerung"

Color palette: prussian blue, black, bone white,
tarnished gold, dark arterial red used sparingly.
The blue is sick, not beautiful.

Style: vintage Japanese magical girl anime illustration
crossed with German expressionist woodcut sensibility,
detailed linework, melancholic dramatic lighting,
collectible poster composition.
