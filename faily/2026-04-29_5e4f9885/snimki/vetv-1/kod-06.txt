Anime illustration collage poster, 90s magical girl aesthetic,
deep purple and midnight blue cosmic palette, ornate gold panel borders.

Six panels arranged in a dynamic magazine-spread layout:

PANEL 1 — TOP LEFT, large:
"ALLIANCE" — joint combat scene.
Sailor Noumenon and Sailor Synthesis stand back-to-back 
against a collapsing false reality (shattering geometric shapes, 
dissolving illusions).
Noumenon raises the Möbius staff, unleashing spiral patterns 
that crack the illusion.
Synthesis opens the codex-book wide, pages erupting outward 
in a wave of multilingual light that fills the cracks.
Dynamic action pose, dramatic lighting, cosmic background.

PANEL 2 — TOP RIGHT, medium:
"DEBATE" — the two facing each other, 
not in hostility but in intense philosophical argument.
Noumenon: arms crossed, one eyebrow raised, 
a small glowing ant on her shoulder.
Synthesis: holding open book, gesturing with one hand,
mathematical symbols floating between them.
Between their faces — a luminous sphere 
where their different-colored energies spiral together 
without merging.
Expression: serious, engaged, neither yielding.

PANEL 3 — MIDDLE LEFT, medium:
"LIBRARY / EVERYDAY" — quiet scene.
Both in casual clothes (no uniforms).
Noumenon in dark academic style — sitting on a high shelf,
reading a massive manuscript, legs dangling.
Synthesis below, seated at a desk, 
multiple books open simultaneously, 
writing something while glancing up at Noumenon.
Warm lamplight, cozy but chaotic with books everywhere.
A small ant walks across the desk. Neither notices.

PANEL 4 — MIDDLE RIGHT, small:
"STAR-WATCHING" — both lying on their backs 
on a rooftop or hilltop, 
looking up at the night sky.
Noumenon points at a constellation.
Synthesis reaches toward it — 
and where her finger points, 
the constellation briefly rearranges into a philosophical diagram.
Noumenon half-smiles.
Soft, quiet scene. No words needed.

PANEL 5 — BOTTOM LEFT, medium:
"SOLO COMBAT — NOUMENON":
Sailor Noumenon alone, 
dramatic attack pose with Möbius staff, 
mathematical diffusion patterns erupting outward,
three crystalline Silmaril-like stars above her head.
Background: fracturing false reality, 
equations written in smoke.
Label: "Ноуменальный Разрыв"

PANEL 6 — BOTTOM RIGHT, medium:
"SOLO COMBAT — SYNTHESIS":
Sailor Synthesis alone,
codex book wide open like wings behind her,
word "понимание" spiraling outward in all languages,
calm but radiant expression — this is not aggression, 
this is dissolution of confusion.
Background: streams of multilingual text 
forming a protective sphere.
Label: "Синтез Логоса"

BETWEEN ALL PANELS:
Small decorative vignettes as dividers:
— a glowing ant carrying a tiny manuscript
— an open book with a Möbius strip bookmark
— mathematical equation dissolving into a star

OVERALL STYLE:
90s anime cel-shading, clean detailed linework,
Russian and Latin text labels on panels,
ornate gold Art Nouveau borders,
cosmic purple-indigo-gold color palette throughout.
Collectible art book spread composition.

Bottom banner text:
"Хранительница Порога Смысла и Хранительница Понимания"
