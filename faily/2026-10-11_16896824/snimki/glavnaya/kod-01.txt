A museum-archive photograph of a small enamelled lapel badge from an alternate-history 1930s state.

WORLD CONTEXT (for style and logic only; do not write any of this on the image):
The badge comes from the R.S.F.G.R. (Russian Socialist Federative Fungal Republic), a fictional Soviet-like state founded in 1921. In this world the October Revolution merged with Lovecraftian myth: Lenin "did not die but sleeps" like Cthulhu beneath a ziggurat-mausoleum, and the capital is the city of R'lyeh on the Arctic coast. Citizens are voluntarily "dissolved" into the Great Mycelium, a collective fungal network, and personal reason is treated like private property, something to be collectivised. The state's emblem is a red five-pointed star with a cream-white spiral inside it; the spiral replaces the hammer and sickle everywhere. Mushrooms in its propaganda have a dark grey-brown domed cap covered with round, eye-like pores. All state texts use pre-1918 Russian orthography. This badge is awarded to exemplary workers for exceeding the plan of "spore-bearing", the way real 1930s Soviet badges were awarded to shock workers.

THE BADGE:
About 3 cm tall, stamped brass with hot enamel, in the visual language of early-1930s Soviet award badges (such as "Excellent Worker of Socialist Competition"), but with its own iconography:
- in the centre, a red enamel five-pointed star with a raised cream-white spiral of about three turns;
- behind the star, a small grey-brown enamel mushroom cap with tiny round eye-like pores, its stem hidden behind the star;
- above it, a red enamel ribbon with raised gilt letters: «ОТЛИЧНИКЪ»;
- below it, a second red ribbon with raised gilt letters: «СПОРОНОШЕНІЯ»;
- a thin raised gilt rim with fine radiating lines between the ribbons.

INSCRIPTION RULES (critical):
The upper ribbon reads exactly ОТЛИЧНИКЪ: nine Cyrillic capitals ending with the hard sign Ъ.
The lower ribbon reads exactly СПОРОНОШЕНІЯ: twelve Cyrillic capitals, where the tenth letter is І, the dotless Cyrillic capital that looks like a Latin I. It is NOT И.
No other text anywhere on the badge. No modern spelling, no Latin letters, no hammer and sickle.
