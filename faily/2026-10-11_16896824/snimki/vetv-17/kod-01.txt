WORLD CONTEXT (for understanding only — do not illustrate it directly):
This is an artifact from a fictional alternate history. In 1921 Russia became the R.S.F.G.R. — a state whose ideology is "healthy madness": its citizens are dissolved into a single vast fungal organism, the Great Mycelium, and the god-like entity Cthulhu is said to sleep beneath an Arctic capital called R'lyeh. A citizen who keeps a separate, private mind is a criminal; minds that are not dissolved are stored outside the body in sealed glass jars ("vessels"). The rest of the world — the "Awake" nations — defends itself not with armies but with sanity: salt, fire and mathematics. In 1963, Professor Ephraim Waite of MIT derived field equations that locally force space back to ordinary Euclidean geometry within a radius of about 500 meters — a zone the Mycelium cannot enter and where its shape-shifting agents cannot keep a human form. This photograph was taken covertly by an R.S.F.G.R. intelligence agent and is now a page in the R.S.F.G.R. state archive. Nothing supernatural is visible in the photo: the dread comes only from what the objects imply.

STYLE AND CONSTRAINTS:
Documentary realism, period-accurate 1963 classroom: no modern objects, no projectors, no whiteboards, no logos. No people, no tentacles, no monsters, no glowing effects, no color except the blue ink and the violet stamp on the white border.
