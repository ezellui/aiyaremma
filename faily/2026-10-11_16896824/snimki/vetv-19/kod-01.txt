WORLD CONTEXT (for correct details):
The object belongs to the archive of the "Р.С.Ф.Г.Р." — an alternate-history Soviet-like state founded in 1921, in which the Communist Party has merged with a cult of the sleeping Cthulhu and with a vast collective fungal organism called the Great Mycelium ("Великая Грибница"). Citizens are ceremonially "dissolved" into the Mycelium; individual reason is considered private property and is condemned. Lenin did not die but "sleeps" beneath his mausoleum, like Cthulhu in R'lyeh; the official motto is "Ктулху фхтагнъ — Ленинъ фхтагнъ". Its emblem is a red five-pointed star with a spiral instead of hammer and sickle; its offices use violet ink stamps with a stylised octopus in the centre. The relevant bureau is the "Department of Spore-breeding and Translations" of the "People's Commissariat of Healthy Madness". This cylinder holds a 1920 conversation between Lenin and the engineer Krzhizhanovsky about electrification, recorded on wax and deciphered only in 1969.
